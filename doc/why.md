# 🛒 Axon Ivy Market

How does the Axon Ivy Market help me write better business solutions?

## 🔌 Connectors

What advantage does writing a connector product provide?
Can't I just use any service-call infrastructure proposed by an AI agent?

The value comes from creating an enterprise-ready component:

- **configurable**: Endpoint URIs, API keys, and the like are designed to be changed in any runtime environment. Connectors use a simple `variables.yaml` file and provide rich UI support at runtime through the Engine Cockpit, including audit trails, deployment overrides, and more.
- **performant**: Connections established by the platform are optimized and battle-tested to work effectively, even at a large scale. You benefit from this power as a connector developer by adhering to our recommended technology stack (e.g., REST clients).
- **monitorable**: Active sessions, problems, and response times are easy to oversee and control with the Engine Cockpit. Poorly performing third parties are therefore easy to spot and manage.
- **authorized**: Establishing a secure connection is prone to errors and can threaten the safety of your application. Connectors provide a secure authentication mechanism that you only need to reuse.

## 💻 Codespace

I could write a good connector infrastructure in my private space.
Is there any benefit to joining the axonivy-market organization?

- **templated**: Our template project comes with good defaults to help you realize your idea faster.
- **reusable**: Products from the axonivy-market are easy to use and install directly from our development environment. The process extension browser makes it easy to include building blocks in your process flow.
- **maintained**: The axonivy-market automates library updates and migrations to other platform versions as much as possible. Changes are created and tested automatically.
- **tested**: Tests are run regularly and for free. This provides feedback on the stability of the shared code, allowing rapid development and changes without breaking existing features.
- **reviewed**: Market products are code that has passed a thorough review by the market maintainers. This quality gate helps developers write better solutions and ensures that harmful products do not reach the market audience.
- **documented**: With minimal effort from the developer, we present and host your documentation for end users. This helps communicate the value and best use of the product.

What's more?

- **components**: Isolated connector products that solve one type of problem are easier to develop, test, and use than a large solution that mixes business logic with connectors.
- **packages**: Product code is built and ready to download. Deployment processes and Maven infrastructure are handled and preconfigured for you.
- **changelogs**: Changes in a new product release are automatically documented and recorded from your code changes. Changelogs are easily accessible to end users on GitHub and the Market website, making them a powerful way to communicate changes to users.
- **examples**: The axonivy-market repositories are a great source to learn from and copy. The developer community and AI assistant can build new products and solutions more rapidly by inspecting the work of others.

All in all, you get a rich, automated ecosystem to handle the full lifecycle of your product.

## 👥 Users

I found an existing product in the market that fits my use case. What do I get from it?

- **speed**: You don't need to worry about the implementation details. Just install the product as a component and quickly build on the work of others.
- **quality**: The product you're installing has gone through a thorough software development cycle. Automated and human feedback systems verify that the product is safe and ready to use.
- **maintenance**: Products are updated and migrated to newer versions without requiring you to touch the code.

## 🏢 Axon Ivy

Do the inventors of the Axon Ivy Platform benefit from the axonivy-market?

- **feedback**: The axonivy-market is a community where we can engage with real users. We love to build what provides real value. Issues and pull requests on GitHub help us stay aligned with you.
- **adoption**: We have built this powerful digital transformation platform for decades. Many features have come and gone during this journey. The Market helps us see which features are actively used. This helps us prioritize our efforts in the areas where most users benefit.
- **loose coupling**: The community builds products, and so do we at Axon Ivy. Having this dedicated space to try, ship, and improve new extensions for the platform is a powerful accelerator. It allows us to ship products with the best-fitting lifecycle.
